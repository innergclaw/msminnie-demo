Our Story

From Pop-Pop's kitchen to your table

Cooking has always been in Ms. Minnie's blood. Growing up, she watched her Pop-Pop run The Rosemont, a community restaurant where people gathered around good food and love. Her father carried that same passion, and it was in those kitchens that she learned not just how to cook, but how to serve people with excellence and heart.

"Every plate reflects our commitment to soulful flavors, family legacy, and professional service."

In 2022, she launched Ms. Minnie's Kitchen & Catering, LLC to continue that legacy. Today, the business offers soulful weekend platters and catering services across Philadelphia, blending tradition with professionalism.

Her vision is to grow into a multi-million-dollar catering company while keeping her roots in community, culture, and authenticity.
